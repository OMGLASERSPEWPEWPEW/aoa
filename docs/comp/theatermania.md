# TheaterMania

**Category:** theater-discovery / deal-ticketing
**URL:** https://www.theatermania.com
**Platforms:** Web
**Founded:** 1999
**Headquarters:** New York, NY
**Status:** Active — smaller than BroadwayWorld/Playbill

---

## What They Do

Theater listings, editorial coverage, reviews, and discount deals aggregated into one site. Positioned as a practical planning tool for theatergoers — find what's playing, read reviews, and discover deals. Less news-focused than BroadwayWorld, more commerce-oriented.

## Target Audience

Theater fans comparing shows before purchasing, especially price-sensitive ticket buyers looking for deals alongside reviews.

**Overlap with AoA:** Low. TheaterMania is a transaction-oriented listing/review site. AoA is a personal companion. Different products.

## Feature Breakdown

| Feature | Details | AoA Overlap |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Show listings | Broadway, Off-Broadway, regional | Adjacent |
| Reviews | Editorial critic reviews | Adjacent — AoA has community reviews |
| Deals/discounts | Discount ticket aggregation | None |
| News | Theater news and features | None |

## Business Model

Advertising, affiliate/ticket referral revenue, sponsored content. Commerce-enabled editorial model.

**Pricing:** Free. Ad-supported.

## Strengths
- Practical utility for planning theater outings
- Reviews + deals create a high-intent audience
- Solid listings coverage

## Weaknesses
- Crowded space — competes with stronger incumbents (BroadwayWorld, Playbill)
- Transaction-oriented, no community
- No map, no social, no tracking, no personalization
- Limited differentiation

## What AoA Can Learn

### Steal This
- **Reviews alongside discovery** — showing reviews at the point of discovery (not in a separate section) helps decision-making

### Differentiation Opportunity
- AoA provides everything TheaterMania doesn't: personalization, social, tracking, map, AI mentorship
