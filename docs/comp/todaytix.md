# TodayTix

**Category:** theater-discovery / deal-ticketing
**URL:** https://www.todaytix.com
**Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web
**Founded:** 2013 (launched December 2013 in NYC)
**Headquarters:** 1501 Broadway, New York, NY 10036
**Status:** Active — acquired by MARI in October 2025
**Founders:** Merritt Baer, Brian Fenty

---

## What They Do

Mobile-first theater and live entertainment ticketing marketplace focused on discounted day-of inventory (Rush and Lottery deals). TodayTix helps users discover shows and buy tickets across Broadway, West End, and other major theater markets, with a strong emphasis on making last-minute theatergoing easy and affordable.

## Target Audience

- Deal-seeking theatergoers who want Broadway/West End tickets at reduced prices
- Last-minute planners who decide same-day
- Regular theatergoers building a habit (rewards program incentivizes repeat purchases)
- NOT newcomers — assumes you already know you want to see theater

**Overlap with AoA:** Medium. TodayTix serves people who already want theater tickets. AoA serves people who don't even know where to start. The discovery layer overlaps, but the intent is different — TodayTix is transactional, AoA is educational/social.

## UX Analysis

### Onboarding
Low-friction — free app, browse immediately. No profile setup required to discover shows. Account needed only for purchase. Time to value is instant: open app, see what's playing.

### Core Loop
Discover show → monitor deal availability → claim Rush or enter Lottery → buy tickets → attend show → return for more. The loop is powered by **scarcity and urgency** — limited inventory, short claim windows, daily refreshes. This creates habitual morning check-ins.

### Map/Discovery UX
TodayTix is NOT map-centric. Discovery is list-based with filters (genre, price, date, location). Shows are organized by market/city, not geographic proximity. No walking-distance discovery, no venue clustering.

### Social Features
Minimal. No friend lists, no activity feeds, no reviews. The rewards program is individual, not social. TodayTix is a transaction tool, not a community.

### Mobile Experience
Mobile-first and optimized for time-sensitive purchases. Fast loading, quick checkout, push notifications for lottery results and rush availability. The app is built around urgency — perfect for "should I see something tonight?" decisions.

## Business Model

| Revenue Stream | Details |
|---------------|---------|
| Commission on ticket sales | Marketplace take-rate on completed transactions |
| Service/convenience fees | Added at checkout (amount varies) |
| B2B venue tools | Widgets, API, white-label solutions sold to venues/producers |
| Promotional partnerships | Venues pay to promote shows in-app |

**Pricing:** Free app. Ticket prices vary by show and deal type. Rush tickets typically $30-50. Lottery tickets typically $30. Regular tickets at market price + fees.

## Strengths

- Strong niche focus on theater specifically (not generic events)
- Rush/Lottery mechanics create addictive daily engagement
- Mobile-first convenience for impulsive same-day decisions
- Broad market coverage including Chicago
- B2B revenue via partner tools creates durability beyond consumer app
- Trust framing — "official tickets" reduces broker skepticism
- Rewards program drives repeat purchases

## Weaknesses

- **Customer service is terrible** — 1.9/5 on PissedConsumer, 2.0/5 on Sitejabber. Common complaints: unreachable support, disconnected phone numbers, ticket access failures, refund disputes
- Scarcity-based frustration — most users lose Rush/Lottery, creating disappointment
- Price uncertainty — total cost depends on show, market, and hidden fees
- No social features — purely transactional, no community
- No educational content — assumes you already know theater
- No personalization beyond rewards tier — doesn't learn your taste deeply
- No map-based discovery — list/filter only

## What AoA Can Learn

### Steal This
- **Rush/Lottery daily engagement mechanic** — The morning check-in habit is powerful. AoA could create a "daily recommendation" or "today's pick" that creates similar habitual app opens
- **Rewards progression tiers** — TodayTix's Bronze/Silver/Gold mirrors AoA's belt system. Study their unlock criteria and messaging
- **"What's playing tonight" urgency** — Same-day discovery is a real use case AoA should support
- **Genre/mood filters** — "Filter by mood" is a great discovery mechanism for newcomers

### Avoid This
- **Customer service vacuum** — TodayTix's terrible support is their biggest weakness. AoA should have clear, responsive support from day one
- **Purely transactional relationship** — TodayTix has zero social/community investment. Users have no loyalty beyond deals
- **No personalization** — TodayTix doesn't learn your taste. AoA's AI mentor is a massive differentiator here

### Differentiation Opportunity
- AoA serves the **pre-TodayTix user** — the person who doesn't even know what shows to look for yet. TodayTix assumes intent; AoA creates it
- AoA has **social tracking and community** — TodayTix has nothing
- AoA has **AI mentorship** — TodayTix has filters. Completely different value prop
- AoA has **progression and education** — TodayTix is a vending machine; AoA is a journey
