# TDF (Theatre Development Fund)

**Category:** deal-ticketing
**URL:** https://www.tdf.org
**Platforms:** Web, Physical TKTS booths
**Founded:** 1968
**Headquarters:** New York, NY
**Status:** Active — 55+ year NYC institution
**Operator:** Nonprofit

---

## What They Do

NYC nonprofit that expands access to theater through discounted ticketing and access programs. Best known for running the **TKTS discount booths** in Times Square, Lincoln Center, and Downtown Brooklyn, which sell same-day Broadway and Off-Broadway tickets at 20-50% off. Also runs accessibility programs, student/educator access, and community engagement initiatives.

## Target Audience

Price-sensitive NYC theatergoers, tourists, students, educators, and arts-access participants.

**Overlap with AoA:** Low. TDF is NYC-specific and focused on ticket discounting. AoA is Chicago-focused and focused on discovery/tracking/mentorship. But TDF's model (nonprofit making theater accessible) aligns with AoA's mission.

## Feature Breakdown

| Feature | Details | AoA Overlap |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| TKTS discount booths | Same-day tickets at 20-50% off | None — AoA doesn't sell tickets |
| Access programs | Student, educator, community access | Adjacent — AoA serves newcomers |

## Business Model

Nonprofit. Revenue from ticket sales/fees and program operations. Mission-driven, not profit-seeking.

## Strengths
- 55+ year institutional brand
- Direct physical presence (TKTS booths)
- Clear public-service mission
- Strong NYC theater ecosystem integration

## Weaknesses
- NYC-only — no relevance outside New York
- Physical-first model in digital world
- Not a discovery or recommendation tool
- No map, no social, no tracking

## What AoA Can Learn

### Steal This
- **Nonprofit mission framing** — TDF's "expanding access" positioning resonates. AoA's mission is similar: making theater less intimidating for newcomers
- **Accessibility programs** — AoA should consider how to serve audiences with disabilities

### Differentiation Opportunity
- AoA is digital-first, Chicago-focused, and goes beyond discounting into mentorship and community
