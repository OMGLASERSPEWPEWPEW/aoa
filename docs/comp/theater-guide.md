## What They Do

National US theater discovery directory that helps users find shows and theater venues across the country. Directory-style browsing with location-based discovery and show/venue information. Broader geographic coverage than city-specific platforms but less depth in any single market.

## Target Audience

Theater fans, travelers, and arts consumers seeking national coverage across US cities.

**Overlap with AoA:** Low. Theater.Guide is a national directory; AoA is a deep Chicago companion. Different approaches to the same domain.

## Business Model

Likely directory-led, traffic-oriented. Monetization unclear from public information.

## Strengths
- National scope — broader reach than local-only platforms
- Simple directory utility

## Weaknesses
- Jack of all cities, master of none — shallow coverage
- No editorial depth, no curation
- No map, no social, no tracking, no AI, no personalization
- Limited traction and unclear sustainability
- Pure directory — no engagement beyond search-and-leave

## What AoA Can Learn

### Steal This
- Nothing significant — Theater.Guide proves that a generic national directory isn't compelling enough

### Avoid This
- **Breadth without depth** — AoA should dominate Chicago before expanding. A thin national layer is worse than a deep local one

### Differentiation Opportunity
- AoA's Chicago depth (venues, AI mentor, community, progression) is infinitely more valuable than a shallow nationwide listing
