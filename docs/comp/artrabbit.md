# ArtRabbit

**Category:** art-culture-discovery
**URL:** https://www.artrabbit.com
**Platforms:** Web, iOS, Android
**Founded:** ~2008
**Headquarters:** London, UK
**Status:** Active — launched artist subscription in 2025

---

## What They Do

Contemporary art discovery platform for finding exhibitions, galleries, openings, festivals, and events. Strong city-by-city coverage with emphasis on planning and curation. More editorial/curated than Artwrld, more established, with a UK/European focus. Recently launched paid artist subscription tiers.

## Target Audience

Contemporary art audiences, artists, collectors, students, researchers, arts professionals. Deeper art-world engagement than Artwrld's casual "open invitation."

**Overlap with AoA:** Low on domain (visual art, not theater), medium on UX model (location-based discovery, follow/save tools, event curation).

## Business Model

Freemium consumer access + paid artist/organization subscriptions (£10/month artist plan). Community-submission-driven database.

**Pricing:** Free for consumers. £10/month for artist profiles.

## Strengths
- Deep niche coverage in contemporary art
- Strong editorial curation
- Location-based discovery with good filtering
- Active community/submission-driven database
- Monetization via artist subscriptions (B2B-ish)

## Weaknesses
- UI/navigation friction reported by users
- Less map-first than Artwrld or Street Art Cities
- UK/European focus — less relevant for US/Chicago
- Visual art only — no performances

## What AoA Can Learn

### Steal This
- **"Closing soon" filter** — surfacing shows about to close creates urgency. AoA should prominently display "closing this weekend" for theater runs
- **Artist subscription model** — AoA could offer paid profiles for theater companies (enhanced venue pages, analytics)

### Differentiation Opportunity
- AoA is theater, not visual art. Different domain, similar UX patterns
- AoA's AI mentor provides personalization ArtRabbit doesn't have
