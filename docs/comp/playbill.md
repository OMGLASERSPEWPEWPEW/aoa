# Playbill

**Category:** theater-discovery
**URL:** https://www.playbill.com
**Platforms:** Web, iOS (Playbill Passport companion app)
**Founded:** 1884 (magazine), digital presence since ~2000s
**Headquarters:** New York, NY
**Status:** Active — iconic theater media brand

---

## What They Do

Historic theater media company that publishes news, show listings, grosses, casting/job listings, and digital program services. Playbill is the physical program you get at Broadway shows, and their digital platform extends that into news, schedules, and show discovery. Recently launched Playbill Passport, an in-theater companion app with archival/memory features.

## Target Audience

Broadway/Off-Broadway fans, theater professionals, industry insiders, and advertisers seeking theater-adjacent audiences.

**Overlap with AoA:** Low. Playbill is institutional theater media. AoA is a personal discovery companion. Different products for different use cases. But Playbill Passport's "scrapbook" features edge closer to AoA's tracking.

## Business Model

Advertising, media sponsorship, ticket referral commerce. Strong brand monetization through physical programs at Broadway theaters.

**Pricing:** Free web access. Ad-supported.

## Strengths
- Iconic 140+ year brand recognition
- Physical presence at every Broadway show
- High traffic and authority in theater media
- Playbill Passport adds personal tracking dimension

## Weaknesses
- Heavy NYC/Broadway concentration
- Not a discovery or recommendation tool
- No map, no AI, no social, no personalization
- Legacy brand — slow to innovate digitally
- Passport app is limited in scope

## What AoA Can Learn

### Steal This
- **Physical program as digital memory** — Playbill Passport captures the "I was there" feeling. AoA should let users save a show entry as a keepsake, not just a data point

### Avoid This
- **NYC tunnel vision** — AoA must stay Chicago-focused and not be drawn to Broadway-centric thinking

### Differentiation Opportunity
- AoA is everything Playbill isn't: interactive, personalized, social, map-based, and designed for newcomers rather than insiders
