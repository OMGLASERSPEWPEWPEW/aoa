# Goldstar

**Category:** deal-ticketing
**URL:** https://www.goldstar.com
**Platforms:** Web, iOS, Android
**Founded:** 2002
**Headquarters:** Pasadena, CA
**Status:** Active — acquired by TodayTix Group in 2022
**Scale:** 10M members, 6,000 venue partners, 26 cities

---

## What They Do

Live-events discovery and discounted ticketing platform offering half-price or better tickets across theater, comedy, concerts, sports, nightlife, food festivals, dance, and more. Goldstar partners directly with venues and event producers to sell inventory that might otherwise go unsold. Acquired by TodayTix Group in 2022, making it a sibling product to TodayTix — Goldstar focuses on broader live entertainment discovery while TodayTix focuses specifically on theater.

## Target Audience

- Price-sensitive entertainment seekers looking for deals across categories
- Casual cultural consumers who want affordable nights out
- Local event explorers (not just theater — all live entertainment)
- Less theater-specific than TodayTix; broader entertainment appeal

**Overlap with AoA:** Low-medium. Goldstar is a deal marketplace; AoA is a discovery/tracking platform. But Goldstar's audience (people looking for affordable cultural experiences) overlaps with AoA's "curious newcomer" who might try theater if the barrier is low enough.

## UX Analysis

### Onboarding
Membership-oriented — sign up for access to deals. Low friction but deal-focused from the start. Immediate value: browse available discounts in your city.

### Core Loop
**Browse deals → find interesting event → purchase discounted ticket → attend → return for more deals.** Similar to HotTix but broader in scope. The loop depends on fresh inventory driving repeat visits — "what's new and cheap this week?"

### Map/Discovery UX
No map. City-based browsing with category filters. Curated feel — positioned as a local events guide rather than a generic ticket search engine. But still fundamentally a deal list, not a discovery experience.

### Social Features
None. Purely transactional.

### Mobile Experience
Apps available on iOS and Android. Deal-browsing optimized for mobile. Standard e-commerce mobile experience.

## Business Model

| Revenue Stream | Details |
|---------------|---------|
| Commission on ticket sales | Marketplace take-rate from venue partnerships |
| Service fees | Added at checkout |

**Pricing:** Free membership. Ticket prices set by venues at discounted rates (typically 50% off or better). Service fees at checkout.

## Strengths

- **Broad event coverage** — not just theater. Comedy, concerts, sports, food, nightlife
- **Strong deal positioning** — "half off or better" is clear value prop
- **10M member base** — substantial audience
- **6,000 venue partnerships** — wide inventory
- **TodayTix Group ownership** — resources and theater expertise
- **Complimentary tickets** — even better than half-price

## Weaknesses

- **Generic deal marketplace** — no curation, no personality, no personalization
- **No tracking or social features** — purely transactional
- **No map** — city-based but not location-aware
- **Inventory-dependent** — only shows discounted events, not everything
- **26 cities only** — limited geographic coverage
- **Fulfillment complexity** — mix of electronic delivery and venue pickup
- **Now owned by TodayTix** — unclear how the brands differentiate going forward

## What AoA Can Learn

### Steal This
- **Multi-category coverage** — Goldstar covers comedy, concerts, dance, food events alongside theater. AoA might consider expanding beyond traditional theater to include improv, comedy, classes, workshops
- **Complimentary ticket partnerships** — Could AoA partner with venues to offer free tickets to first-timers? Powerful onboarding tool
- **"Events near you" email digests** — Weekly deal digests drive habitual engagement

### Avoid This
- **Generic marketplace** — Goldstar has no personality, no curation, no soul. AoA must have all three
- **Deal-only lens** — Like HotTix, showing only discounted events misses the full picture
- **No retention beyond deals** — Once the deal is done, there's no reason to come back

### Differentiation Opportunity
- AoA is the **discovery and relationship layer** that deal platforms like Goldstar lack
- AoA could **surface Goldstar/TodayTix deals** as a data point within recommendations ("your mentor suggests X, and it's 50% off on Goldstar")
- AoA serves users **before they know what they want** — Goldstar serves users who already decided to go out
