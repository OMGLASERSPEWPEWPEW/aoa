## What They Do

Theater ratings aggregator that consolidated audience and critic scores into a single "Show-Score" to help users quickly decide what to see. Functioned as a Rotten Tomatoes for theater — simplifying show quality assessment into one number.

## Target Audience

Theatergoers seeking quick quality signals rather than reading full reviews. Decision-stage users comparing shows.

**Overlap with AoA:** Low-medium. The ratings aggregation concept is relevant (AoA has community ratings), but Show-Score appears inactive.

## Feature Breakdown

| Feature | Details | AoA Overlap |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Aggregated scores | Consolidated critic + audience ratings | Adjacent — AoA community ratings |
| Show comparison | Browse shows by score | Adjacent — AoA could rank by community rating |
| Review aggregation | Pulled reviews from multiple sources | None — AoA generates its own reviews |

## Business Model

Likely advertising and affiliate ticket links. No subscription model evident.

## Strengths
- Simple value proposition — one score tells you if a show is good
- Reduced decision paralysis

## Weaknesses
- Ratings aggregators have weak network effects
- Limited differentiation
- Dependent on fresh coverage
- Appears to have lost traction / possibly defunct

## What AoA Can Learn

### Steal This
- **Aggregate community score** — A single community rating number (like "87% of AoA users loved this") on show cards would help discovery decisions

### Avoid This
- **Single-metric products are fragile** — Show-Score had one feature and apparently couldn't sustain it. AoA must be a platform, not a feature
