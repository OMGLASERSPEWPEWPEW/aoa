# User Journey: The Art of Art

**Date:** 2026-07-30

---

## Belt Progression Model

Inspired by martial arts ranking. Each belt gates content and features — not to restrict, but to prevent overwhelm and create a sense of earned discovery.

| Belt | Color | Name | Criteria | Unlocks |
|------|-------|------|----------|---------|
| 0 | **White** | Observer | Sign up, complete onboarding (age, city, interests) | Map, mentor chat, venue profiles, "want to see" list, learning modals |
| 1 | **Yellow** | First Timer | See 1 show, write 1 brief reflection | Reviews tab, community ratings visible, "shows seen" counter |
| 2 | **Orange** | Explorer | See 3 shows across 2+ venues, read 3 learning modules | Full review writing, social features (add friends, see activity) |
| 3 | **Green** | Regular | See 6 shows across 3+ venues, write 3 reviews, complete genre quiz | Recommendation engine ("because you liked X"), advanced filters |
| 4 | **Blue** | Enthusiast | See 12 shows in a season, try 2 different genres, attend 1 opening night | Opening night alerts, "hidden gem" recommendations, mentor deep dives |
| 5 | **Purple** | Advocate | Invite 2 friends who see a show, write 5+ reviews, usher once | Community leader badge, ability to create curated lists for others |
| 6 | **Brown** | Connoisseur | See 25+ shows, attend 3+ different theaters in a season, engage with 5+ learning modules | Full critic tools, theater company follow, season planning |
| 7 | **Black** | Patron | Sustained engagement over 2+ seasons, active community contributor | All features, mentor treats you as peer, can mentor new users |

### Belt Design Principles

1. **Criteria are actions, not time.** You advance by doing things, not waiting.
2. **No criteria require spending money.** Ushering is free. Reflection doesn't cost anything.
3. **Social criteria appear only at Orange and above.** New users aren't pressured to invite friends.
4. **Each belt unlocks something genuinely valuable**, not just a badge.
5. **Belts are visible on profiles** but not competitive. No leaderboards. This is personal growth.

## Level 0: First Contact (White Belt)

### Discovery
- User finds the app through acting class word-of-mouth, social media, or app store search
- Landing page: map with glowing theater markers + mentor character waving

### First 30 Seconds
1. Mentor appears with speech bubble: introduces themselves, asks "What brought you here?"
2. User selects from options: "Taking acting classes," "Want to see more plays," "New to the city," "Just curious"
3. Mentor responds with personality (not generic): "Acting classes! You're going to love this..."

### Onboarding (2 minutes max)
1. **Age range**: 20s / 30s / 40s / 50s+ (drives life-stage recommendations)
2. **City**: Chicago (launch city, expandable later)
3. **Experience level**: "Never seen a play" / "Seen a few" / "Go regularly" / "Theater professional"
4. **Interests** (pick 3): Comedy, Drama, Musicals, Experimental, Shakespeare, New Works, Physical Theater, Improv
5. Mentor summarizes: "Got it. Here's what I think you should do first..."

### First Action
- Mentor recommends ONE specific show to see this week, with explanation of why
- Map zooms to that theater with a glowing marker
- "Want to See" button appears — one tap to add to watchlist
- HotTix link if available for that show

### First Value
- The user now has a concrete, personalized recommendation with context
- They know ONE thing to do this weekend
- The map shows them where it is
- They feel like someone who knows the scene just gave them advice

## Level 1: Early Engagement (Yellow Belt)

### What Brings Them Back
- Push notification: "How was [show name]? Tap to reflect." (after event date)
- Mentor checks in: "Hey, did you make it to [show]?"
- New "recommended for you" shows appear on their map

### After First Show
- User taps "I saw this" on the show in their watchlist
- Mentor asks: "What did you think? Quick or detailed?"
  - Quick: 1-5 star rating + optional one-line
  - Detailed: guided reflection prompts ("What surprised you?" "Would you recommend it?")
- Badge animation: Yellow belt earned

### What They Learn
- Learning modal unlocks: "How Chicago Theater Works" (storefront ecosystem explainer)
- Mentor drops knowledge naturally in chat: "Fun fact — the theater you just went to was founded by..."
- "Shows Like This" recommendations based on their first rating

---

## Level 2-3: Building the Habit (Orange → Green)

### Orange (Explorer)
- User is now seeing shows with some regularity
- Social features unlock: can add friends, see friends' activity
- "Want to see" list grows — becomes their personal theater calendar
- Mentor gets smarter: "I noticed you've liked the storefront shows more than the big houses..."
- Community ratings become visible on all shows

### Green (Regular)
- User has opinions now. They know what they like.
- Recommendation engine activates: "Because you loved [X] at [Y], you should see [Z]"
- Advanced map filters: by genre, price, neighborhood, date, venue size
- Genre quiz unlocks: fun interactive way to discover their taste profile
- Mentor shifts tone: less guiding, more conversational

---

## Level 4-5: Deepening (Blue → Purple)

### Blue (Enthusiast)
- Opening night alerts for followed companies
- "Hidden gem" recommendations — smaller shows they might miss
- Mentor deep dives: longer conversations about playwrights, movements, history
- Can follow specific theater companies for updates

### Purple (Advocate)
- Community leader features: create curated "lists" (like Letterboxd lists)
- Can invite friends with personalized onboarding: "Deric thinks you'd love..."
- Mentor acknowledges their knowledge: "You probably already know this, but..."
- Ushering tracker and volunteer opportunities

---

## Level 6-7: Mastery (Brown → Black)

### Brown (Connoisseur)
- Season planning tools: map out a full season across companies
- Theater company comparison views
- Full critic-style review tools (longer form, structured)
- Mentor engages as a peer: "What do you think about [trend]?"

### Black (Patron)
- Can opt into mentoring new White Belt users (peer mentoring within the app)
- Mentor treats them as an equal: collaborative discovery
- Legacy features: "Your theater journey" annual recap
- All restrictions removed

---

## Power User (6 months in)

A power user:
- Opens the app 3-4x/week to check what's playing
- Has a running "want to see" list of 10-15 shows
- Rates every show they see
- Has 5-10 friends on the platform
- Reads learning modules occasionally
- Chats with the mentor for recommendations before weekend plans
- Has brought 2-3 friends to their first show through the app
- Considers themselves "a theater person" — the app helped make that identity shift
